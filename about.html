About DSPACE

Built on Access, Not Inventory.

DSPACE was founded to close the gap between global capital and the two real estate markets it consistently underestimates: the UAE and India.

03
02 — Our Story

Founded in Dubai.
Built for two markets.

DSPACE was founded in 2014 by a small group of advisors who had spent years watching international clients get underserved on both ends of the Dubai–India corridor — sold inventory by one side, and shut out of off-market opportunity on the other.

We built DSPACE as a single advisory spanning both markets: one point of contact, one standard of diligence, whether the asset is a beachfront villa on Palm Jumeirah or a heritage bungalow in Bandra. Today we work with a deliberately limited roster of private clients, family offices and developers each year.
